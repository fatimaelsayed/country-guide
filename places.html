Where to go

Places To Visit

Scotland has an incredibly rich history, dramatic landscapes, and vibrant culture. From medieval cobble streets to misty rock formations and ancient castles, each destination captures the unique beauty of the Highlands and beyond.

Historic Landmark

Edinburgh & The Royal Mile

Scotland's historic capital boasts medieval cobbled streets, Georgian architecture, and Edinburgh Castle perched atop an extinct volcano.

Natural Wonder

The Isle of Skye

Famous for its breathtaking landscapes, the Isle of Skye features dramatic mountain scenery, picturesque fishing villages, and ancient castles.

Mystical Loch

Loch Ness & Urquhart Castle

Deep in the Scottish Highlands lies Loch Ness, world-famous for its mythical monster Nessie, sweeping valleys, and castle ruins.

Glacial Valley

Glencoe Valley

A deeply dramatic glacial valley framed by towering, steep-sided mountains, famous for its haunting history and breathtaking hiking trails.

Architectural Icon

Glenfinnan Viaduct

An iconic 21-arch railway bridge set against a stunning backdrop of lochs and hills, world-renowned for its appearance in the Harry Potter films.

Coastal Town

St Andrews

A historic coastal town known worldwide as the home of golf, featuring medieval ruins, a prestigious ancient university, and scenic beaches.
