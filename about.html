About Scotland

Scotland is a country that is part of the United Kingdom, consisting of the northern part of the island of Great Britain and adjacent islands, principally in the archipelagos of the Hebrides and the Northern Isles.

The country splits roughly into three regions: the Lowlands, where most people live, the Highlands — a vast stretch of mountains and lochs in the north — and around 800 islands off the coast.

Capital Edinburgh
Population ~5.5 million
Language English, Scots, Gaelic
